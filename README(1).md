# Netflix Sales Dashboard using Excel

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyses Netflix sales and subscription data using
Microsoft Excel. An interactive dashboard was developed to monitor
revenue, active users, watch hours, subscription performance, regional
performance, and new subscriptions versus cancellations.

## 🎯 Project Objective

-   Analyse Netflix sales and subscription performance.
-   Track key business KPIs.
-   Identify revenue trends across years and countries.
-   Compare performance across subscription types and regions.
-   Analyse user activity and watch-hour patterns.
-   Compare new subscriptions with cancellations.
-   Present findings through an interactive Excel dashboard.

## 🗂️ Dataset Overview

The dataset contains **10,956 records** and **13 attributes** and covers
**2021 to 2025**.

### Key Attributes

Date, Year, Month, Quarter, Country, Region, Subscription Type, Device,
New Subscriptions, Cancellations, Active Users, Monthly Revenue (USD),
and Watch Hours.

## 📊 Dashboard Analysis

The workbook contains analysis sheets covering:

1.  **Revenue Trend by Year** --- analyses total monthly revenue across
    2021--2025.
2.  **Revenue by Country** --- compares monthly revenue across
    countries.
3.  **Subscription Type Analysis** --- compares revenue from Basic,
    Mobile, Premium, and Standard plans.
4.  **Quarterly Growth Analysis** --- analyses revenue across quarters
    and subscription types.
5.  **Top Region by Active Users** --- compares active users across
    regions.
6.  **New vs Cancellation Analysis** --- compares new subscriptions with
    cancellations.
7.  **Watch Hour Analysis** --- examines watch-hour patterns across
    years and months.

  KPI                        Value

  Total Revenue          \$154.00M

  Cancellations              3.34M

## 🔍 Key Insights

-   Total revenue across the analysed period was approximately
    **\$154.00 million**.
-   **2025** recorded the highest annual revenue among the years
    analysed, at approximately **\$31.02 million**.
-   **Premium** subscription generated the highest revenue among the
    subscription types, at approximately **\$66.62 million**.
-   **Europe** recorded the highest total active users among the regions
    analysed, with approximately **221.73 million** active users.
-   New subscriptions (**14.22 million**) were substantially higher than
    cancellations (**3.34 million**) in the KPI analysis.
-   The dataset contains approximately **2.86 billion watch hours**
    across the analysed records.
